# Privacy

The public demo's notice is available at [tudla-demo.vercel.app/privacy.html](https://tudla-demo.vercel.app/privacy.html). Its source is [apps/web/public/privacy.html](apps/web/public/privacy.html).

The demo stores records and unfinished campaign drafts in browser local storage. Hosting providers still process technical request information to deliver and protect the site. Contact email and external links are separate services. The notice explains these boundaries and how to clear browser-local records.

A self-hosted authenticated installation has different data flows and retention. Its operator must provide an appropriate notice and contact route for that installation; the public demo notice does not describe another operator's service. See [Deployment](docs/DEPLOYMENT.md).
