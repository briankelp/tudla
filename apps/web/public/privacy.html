Privacy

Updated 28 October 2026

This notice describes the public demo at tudla-demo.vercel.app. It uses sample businesses and does not require an account.

Records stay in your browser

Campaigns, imported CSV records, prospect notes, email drafts, and unfinished campaign drafts are stored in this browser's local storage. The demo does not send those records to an application server, database, or AI provider. It includes no application analytics or tracking cookies.

Local storage is not encrypted by Tudla and can be accessed by the browser profile. Use sample records rather than credentials, private contacts, or sensitive information.

Clear your data

Clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove its saved records and drafts. Clearing browser storage cannot remove CSVs you exported, copied text, or messages opened in another application. Those follow your device and service settings.

Hosting and external services

Vercel hosts the demo and may process technical request information, including IP addresses, for delivery and security. Its handling and retention are described in Vercel's privacy notice.

GitHub, business websites, and your email application have their own privacy practices. Opening an email draft hands its contents to your email application; you decide whether to send it. If you contact the maintainer, your email and anything you include are processed to respond to your message.

Self-hosted installations

An authenticated installation can store records on its operator's server and send data to enabled providers. This demo notice does not describe those deployments. Ask that installation's operator about its privacy, access, and deletion procedures.
